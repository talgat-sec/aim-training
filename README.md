# Click Training — Browser Aim Trainer

A free browser-based aim and reaction trainer for a quick warm-up before games such as Counter-Strike 2, Call of Duty, VALORANT, Apex Legends, and Fortnite.

**[Play Click Training](https://aim-training-online.netlify.app/)**

## Features

- Click moving targets to practice accuracy and reaction speed.
- See the time left and your score live in the corner of the arena while you play.
- Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty.
- Set a custom session length or use a preset from 30 seconds to 5 minutes (1.5 minutes by default).
- Switch between English and Russian; toggle sound on or off.
- Play directly in your browser without installing anything.
- Save a completed round under a nickname and view the top 10 for each duration and difficulty. No account is required.

## How to play

1. Open the [live game](https://aim-training-online.netlify.app/).
2. Choose a session length and difficulty.
3. Select **Start New Game** and click the targets as they appear.
4. Review your results when the session ends, or press **Esc** to end it early.
5. After a full round, enter a nickname and press Enter or select **Save Score**. The top 10 for that duration and difficulty appears right in the results card, with your row highlighted. Open **Leaderboard** to browse other modes.

## Built with

HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The live site is hosted on Netlify. Leaderboard data is stored with Netlify Blobs through a Netlify Function.

Scores are submitted from the browser, so the leaderboard is intended for casual competition.
